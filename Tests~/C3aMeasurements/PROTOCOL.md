## Counters, validation, cleanup, and reporting

No forced GC. Setup, fixture precheck, and64 warmup workflows are outside counters. Retained restoration is inside counters; new public construction/build/growth is inside counters. Count/last-name consumer is inside, except the lifecycle retains its exact historical consumer. Never subtract reset, observer, control, or component medians. Do not call retained copy a pure CopyFrom kernel.

Every numerical sample includes actual calls, Stopwatch ticks/frequency/duration, ns/op, total/per-op allocated bytes, GC0/1/2 deltas, sink, geometry, and short flag. The numeric JSON line is flushed before postvalidation/reflection/capacity census. Postvalidation records actual result count/capacity/reference-array payload and source/seed capacities; lifecycle also records union/intersection counts/capacities. Each disposable new result is consumed; full postvalidation observes the final result, while all fixture shapes have their separate untimed precheck.
