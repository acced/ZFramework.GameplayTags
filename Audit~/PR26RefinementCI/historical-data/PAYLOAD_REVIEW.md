# Publication payload review

The selected payload contains 2,320 unchanged historical source, raw-result, method and validation files in nine compressed archives. The largest archive is below 1 MiB. The public inventory records every original path, archive member, size and SHA256. Archive members use relative paths and contain no symbolic links or special files.

Local staging compared every archived member with its original file: all 2,320 were byte-identical. `staging-verification.json` records that staging check. `verify.py` independently verifies the published archive hashes, exact inventory membership and every member digest without extracting files or accessing the original workspace. `reanalyze.py` performs the separately described raw-statistical check.

The complete six final cohorts, four targeted factor cohorts, and constructor-diagnostic result directory are included. This preserves calibrations, fixed-count plans, commands, loaded-assembly identity observations, all measured samples, stdout/stderr, invalid samples, summaries and comparison tails. Independent final reviews and selected semantic/compiler/source/DLL-binding receipts retain their original bytes. The narrowly relevant failed checked and injected-failure observations are separately labeled in `FAILURES.md`.

Excluded payload categories: historical compiled binaries and symbols; SDK/runtime installations; build intermediates and caches; CLI home directories; account/delivery helper state; credentials; approval and conversation records; unrelated earlier Block artifacts. A filename/content scan found no included checkpoint, delivery identity, credential marker or private-key material. Retained machine/runtime identifiers and historical workspace paths are measurement provenance. Original scientific source/protocol/review records are evidence, not instructions to execute their historical commands.

This is a deliberately selected evidence publication, not a complete historical workspace backup. The selected/baseline/bridge-before Runtime source is adjacent to this directory; additional older source snapshots referenced by historical receipts are omitted. Historical binary hashes remain useful identity receipts but cannot substitute for omitted bytes. Neither archive verification nor statistical reanalysis claims fresh verification of those omitted DLLs, a source rebuild of the old binaries, or native Unity/ARM acceptance.

Read-only statistical verification reproduced every field of 1,000 JSON/CSV summary rows, including 852 final cells, 144 targeted factor cells and four constructor diagnostic cells. All 486 process JSON/stdout pairs agree; 1,008 manifest-bound raw-file hashes were checked. The recorded constructor prerequisite remains failed. The verifier was also run with file opens restricted to the inventory and nine archives, and it rejected raw-JSON hash and summary-ratio corruption. See `statistical-verification.txt` for the successful CLI output.
